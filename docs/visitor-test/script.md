# Visitor test: the script (one page)

For the person running the session. Five people who do not work with data, one at a time,
about 20 minutes each. Half on a phone, half on a laptop. One copy of `form.md` per person.

**No one has been tested yet.** Nothing here says the site passes.

## Before the person sits down

- Open the site's home page. Nothing else open, no saved canvases from an earlier person
  (use a private window, or clear the site's data first).
- Have the form and a timer ready. Do not record names: write "person 1" to "person 5".

## Say this, as written

> Thanks for helping. This is a website about vehicles registered in New Zealand. I am testing
> the website, not you: if something is hard to find, that is the site's fault and exactly
> what I need to see.
>
> I will read out five questions, one at a time. Use the site to answer each one. Please think
> out loud as you go: say what you are looking for and what you expect to happen.
>
> I can't help or answer questions while you work, but I will note them down. Tell me when you
> have an answer, or when you would give up if you were on your own.

## The rules for you

- Do not point, hint, name a page or read the screen to them. If they ask, say "what would
  you do if I were not here?"
- Start the timer when you finish reading a task aloud. Stop it when they state an answer.
- After each answer, ask: **"What is that figure counting?"** Write down their words.
- If they are stuck for two minutes, say "let's move on" and mark it as not finished.
- Put them back on the home page before each task.

## The five tasks

| # | Read aloud | Start from |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "How many vehicles were registered last month, and is that up or down on a year ago?" | the home page |
| 2 | "Which brands lead, and who is gaining or losing?" | the home page |
| 3 | "What share are electric, plug-in and hybrid vehicles now, and how has that changed?" | the home page |
| 4 | "What is happening in your region?" | the home page |
| 5 | "Among Ford, Toyota and BYD only, which brand has the most registrations, for one powertrain and one vehicle segment of your choice?" Then: "Now send me that as a link." | the home page |

Tasks 1 to 4 are starter questions 1, 2, 4 and 9 of `docs/brief/MASTER_BRIEF.md` §2. Task 5 is
the acceptance question. To swap one in, take another from that list and say so on the form.

## What counts as a pass

Tasks 1 to 4 pass when all three are true:

1. **Unaided:** they reached an answer with no help from you.
2. **Under a minute,** from the end of the question to a stated answer.
3. **They say correctly what the figure counts,** in their own words, as the page says it
   (for example vehicles first registered in a month, not sales). A right number with a wrong
   account of what it counts is a fail.

Task 5 passes when they build the chart unaided, state an answer, say what it counts, and
produce a link. There is no time limit for task 5: record the time.

The site's measure is 4 of 5 people passing each task (`docs/brief/MASTER_BRIEF.md` §1).

## At the end, ask

- "What was the hardest part?"
- "Was there a word on the site you did not understand?"
- "Would you trust these figures? What would make you trust them more, or less?"

## Afterwards

Give the five forms to Claude (photos or typed). Each finding is listed once with how many
people hit it; what can be fixed is fixed, and the rest goes in `docs/CHECKPOINT.md` with the
evidence.
